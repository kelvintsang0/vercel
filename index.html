Real problems,
practical fixes.

"Two dashboards, two different numbers. And we have a policy for that."
A policy on its own isn't governance. Someone has to decide what the numbers mean, write that decision down, and own what happens when it's wrong.
Watch: what data governance really means →
"Everyone uses this data. Nobody owns it."
The answer is almost never IT. Find the person who gets to decide, and lead with the decision rights, not the extra work.
Watch: who actually owns your data? →
"Leadership asks: will governance slow us down?"
That's the wrong question. Ask what the business can't do today because of its data, and sell the answer to that.

About
Kelvin Tsang
CDMP Practitioner · PMP · MBA · PSM I

I'm a data governance practitioner based in Vancouver, BC, with 20+ years in enterprise data across investment management, entertainment and transportation, at institutions where governance wasn't optional. It was regulatory and reputational.

As VP, Chief Data Office at J.P. Morgan Asset Management in Hong Kong, I led data governance across Asia Pacific, aligning cross-border privacy rules, audit frameworks and operational reporting. I was promoted three times over 10 years.

At AIMCo, I directed the enterprise rollout of Atlan and Soda, designed data quality scorecards and stewardship operating models, and led a 10-person cross-functional data product delivery team, moving into management within two months of joining.

Today I lead data infrastructure and governance at a Canadian transportation organization, building a modern data platform on Microsoft Fabric and Purview.

I hold the CDMP Practitioner designation, PMP, an MBA from Schulich (York University), an Honours BSc in Computer Science from UBC and an MIT certificate in AI product design, and I'm completing the AI Governance Professional (AIGP) certification. I also teach data management on Udemy and share what I've learned on The Practical Steward.
